FAQ

Questions before you add a card

Why do you need my card if the first month is free?

The free month runs on real paid models, billed from your 1,000 credits. The card keeps it to one free month per account, and Basic continues without a gap if you stay. Nothing is charged today.

When will I be charged?

Not today. After 30 days, Basic renews automatically at $20/month until you cancel. Cancel anytime before it renews and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

In your account on quadcode.ai, under Billing. You can cancel any time; to avoid the $20 charge, cancel before the free month renews.

What's included in the free month?

Basic, 1,000 credits for the first month, all platform features, and the desktop app for macOS and Windows. Basic credits roll over for 1 month.

What if I use up my credits?

You can route heavy coding to your own Claude Code or Codex subscription, use local models (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) or any OpenAI-compatible API, or pick cheaper models for drafts.

Which models can I use?

Code models: GPT, Claude (Opus, Sonnet), Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, plus local models (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM). Image, video, voice and music models include GPT Image, Nano Banana, Flux, Luma, ElevenLabs and Suno.

Does it run on Mac and Windows?

Yes. Quadcode AI is a desktop app for macOS and Windows. You can start the free month on the website first and install the app afterwards.

Do I have to give up Claude Code or Codex?

No. Quadcode AI runs Claude Code, Codex and other CLI agents as subagents inside the app, on your own subscription, and adds media generation, specialist agents and project tooling on top.

The card form says my card was already used. What now?

Each account gets one free month. Try a different card, or contact support@quadcode.ai.

Can I just pay for a bigger plan?

Yes. Pro has 5,000 credits a month for $100/month, or $75/month billed annually ($900/year). Enterprise volumes are set with the team.
